# The Roommate Who Eats Nothing

Entry for [One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521) — one line roasting the fridge, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The roast

> My fridge is the only roommate who eats nothing, makes noise all night, and still expects me to pay the electricity bill.

121 characters. The fridge becomes the worst roommate imaginable: it contributes nothing, hums all night, and still sends you the bill. The twist lands in the last few words — the appliance that does the least expects you to pay for the privilege — and the line reads cleanly aloud in one breath, with a specific, recognisable detail (the all-night hum) instead of a generic insult.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the roast, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
